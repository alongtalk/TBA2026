Team Placement

We build classes, not teams. Tryouts are for 22 players per grade level.

Nov 1 — Tryouts
Held the final weekend of the Academy. We are looking for 22 players per grade level.
Nov & Dec — Placement Practices
Multiple practices determine final team placement for each player for within the grade.
Two Teams per Grade
Each grade level fields two teams. Teams practice together twice a week, two hours per session, and travel together to games.
Season Timeline

November 2026 – July 2027

Nov – Jan · Local Season

No fall or winter travel circuit. Teams build together with regular practices and play locally — often against older grade levels — through January.

Feb – Jun · Spring/Summer Travel Circuit

4th, 5th, and 6th grade teams compete on spring circuits, with additional travel tournaments scheduled. 2nd & 3rd grade teams participate in select travel tournaments as well.

Jul · Championship Season

All grades participate in AAU and/or Junior EYBL circuit national championships.
