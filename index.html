About the Program

Trust the process. Decades of success.

In an effort to continue to provide the best possible basketball development for players in 3rd–6th grade, Team Takeover is setting the pace in grassroots basketball by continuing the Takeover Basketball Academy.

First launched in 2019, TBA returned in 2023, 2024 & 2025 and delivered an AMAZING experience with over 100 players and families each year. We recognize that the fall "off" season has become a mess — wrought with crazy extra costs, uncertain schedules, and uninspiring competition.

The TBA is built to change that. A structured, purposeful fall program that prepares your child for the AAU season ahead — not just gets them through it.
